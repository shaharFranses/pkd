Bakery Logic Puzzle

One cake in every row, column and patch of dough, and no two cakes touching. A calm, cozy logic puzzle.

play full screen ▶ open in a new tab ↗

how to play

  • Tap a cell to mark an empty mold, tap again to place a cake, tap again to clear.
  • Every row, column and color patch gets exactly one cake.
  • Cakes can't touch, not even diagonally, or they'd stick together in the oven.

no rush

No timer and no fail state. Every board has exactly one solution, reachable by logic alone. Finish a chapter to unlock a recipe card.
