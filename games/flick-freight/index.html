Flick Freight

Drag back, let go, and flick cargo from the dock into the truck. Stack it full without toppling it.

play full screen ▶ open in a new tab ↗

how to play

  • Press anywhere, drag back, release. The dotted arc previews the throw.
  • Load enough cargo to pass; load it all for 3 stars.
  • P pauses, R restarts. Touch works the same way.

what's inside

96 levels across 9 worlds: moving trucks, fragile cargo, wind, bounce pads, icy truck beds and more, introduced one at a time.
